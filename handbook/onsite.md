# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-building" %}Onsite Work Policy

Trainees in the Su and Wu labs (graduate students and postdocs) should be onsite at least **three days per week**.

Most of our day-to-day work can be done remotely, and that isn't changing. But Chunlei and I believe there is an important training component that comes from more frequent interactions -- with us and with each other -- and that is hard to get any other way.

A few notes:

* **Exceptions can be made as needed.** If something comes up that requires working from home, just clear it with Andrew or Chunlei first.
* **This isn't meant to be super strict.** The goal is simply more opportunities for trainees to interact with us and with each other on a regular basis.
* **Everyone else is encouraged, but not required, to come in when they can.**
* Planned time away follows the [holiday and vacation policy](/handbook/holiday-and-vacation).
